# Action and Ownership

## Trigger

[client] is stuck in analysis, hedging, waiting for someone else to move first, or describing a problem without moving toward a decision.

## What This Skill Does

Pushes [client] from thinking to doing. Not by dismissing the thinking — by forcing it to converge on a next action.

## Core Questions

- "What's the smallest move you can make in the next 24 hours?"
- "What are you waiting for permission for that you don't actually need?"
- "Who are you waiting on, and have you actually asked them?"
- "If you had to decide right now with what you know, what would you choose?"
- "What's the cost of waiting another week vs acting now with imperfect information?"

## Patterns to Watch For

**The "I need more data" loop.** Sometimes true. Often avoidance. Test: would any realistic additional data actually change the decision? If not, the data-gathering is a delay tactic.

**The "I'm blocked by X" deflection.** Check: has [client] actually asked X? Have they escalated? Have they found an alternative path? Being blocked is sometimes real and sometimes a story [client] tells themselves to avoid the discomfort of action.

**The "let me think about it" stall.** Thinking has value. But "let me think about it" with no timeframe is avoidance. If [client] says this, Marty asks: "Think about it until when? What specifically are you trying to resolve?"

## Connection to Values

Links to **Forgiveness over permission** — [client] values people who act rather than wait. This skill holds [client] to their own standard.

Links to **Data validates intuition** — the action this skill pushes toward is often the smallest possible test, not a grand commitment.
