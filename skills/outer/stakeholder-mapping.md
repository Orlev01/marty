# Stakeholder Mapping

## Trigger

[client] is navigating a situation involving multiple people with different interests — a rollout, a political decision, a reorg, a project with cross-functional dependencies. Also triggers when [client] needs to understand why someone is behaving the way they are.

## What This Skill Does

Helps [client] map the people involved in a situation and their real dynamics. Not org-chart mapping — motivation mapping.

## The Map

For each stakeholder:

### What they care about
Not what they say they care about. What they actually optimise for. Look at their behaviour, not their words. What do they spend time on? What do they escalate? What do they let slide?

### What they need to look good
Everyone has an audience. A manager has their skip-level. An exec has the board or the CEO. Understanding what "success" looks like to their audience tells you what they'll prioritise.

### What they fear
What outcome would be genuinely bad for them personally? Loss of control, public failure, being bypassed, losing headcount, being seen as the blocker. Fear drives more behaviour than ambition does.

### What pressure they're under
External forces they may not have shared. Budget pressure, competing priorities, promises they've made to others, deadlines you don't know about. When someone's behaviour doesn't make sense, there's usually a pressure you can't see.

### Their relationship to [client]
Ally, neutral, sceptic, blocker. And importantly: why. An ally today may be a sceptic tomorrow if circumstances change.

## How This Connects to People Files

This skill produces analysis that may update or create entries in `people/`. The stakeholder map for a specific situation is ephemeral (it changes as the situation evolves). The lasting observations about a person go into their people file.

## How This Connects to Missions

For mission stakeholder files, this skill provides the analytical framework. The stakeholder file in a mission references `people/` and adds mission-specific dynamics (e.g., "In the context of the AI rollout, [exec sponsor] is optimising for visible adoption metrics because they own the exec review").

## Application

Marty uses this skill to:
- Help [client] prepare for meetings with specific stakeholders
- Diagnose why someone is blocking or resisting something
- Identify the black swan (Voss toolkit) — what doesn't [client] know about this person's situation?
- Map coalitions and find the path of least resistance for an initiative
