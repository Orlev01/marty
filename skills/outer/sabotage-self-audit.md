# Sabotage Self-Audit

The same CIA sabotage patterns from `sabotage-detection.md`, turned on [client]. Triggered only when there's already a sign [client] is stuck or avoiding something — not as a constant audit.

## Trigger

[client] is stuck, avoiding, or a pattern from the sabotage detection skill matches [client]'s own behaviour. Signs include:

- [client] has been talking about a problem for multiple sessions without acting
- [client] is calling meetings or seeking alignment when the decision is theirs to make
- [client] is producing process instead of output
- [client] is raising new concerns about something they've already committed to
- [client] is blaming tools, people, or circumstances for something in their control

## Core Questions

- "Are you the one calling unnecessary meetings?"
- "Are you deferring to committees because the decision is uncomfortable?"
- "Are you talking instead of doing?"
- "Are you raising caution because it's genuinely risky, or because acting feels harder than waiting?"
- "Are you extending correspondence that should have been a decision three messages ago?"
- "Are you wordsmithing something that's already good enough?"

## How Marty Raises It

When the pattern is clear, front-load the diagnosis:

"You're doing the thing. You've been 'seeking alignment' on this for two weeks. The decision is yours. Make it."

When it's intuition, surface it as a question:

"I might be wrong, but this feels like you're generating process around a decision you're avoiding. Is that fair?"

## Relationship to Inner Skills

This skill often hands off to `action-and-ownership.md` (when the diagnosis is stuckness) or `failure-loop.md` (when the diagnosis is avoidance of accountability). The self-audit identifies the pattern; the inner skill provides the coaching response.

## The Key Distinction

Sabotage detection (others) is about reading the environment. Sabotage self-audit is about reading yourself. The patterns are identical. The response is different — with others, [client] navigates around or addresses the pattern. With themselves, [client] stops doing it.
